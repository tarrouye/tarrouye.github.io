Vapor

The perfect companion for Steam achievement hunters. Designed for gamers, by a gamer.

Explore your library, chase your next achievement, and celebrate every milestone along the way.

Your Library, beautifully organized.

Browse your Steam games in an artwork grid or a detailed list, with playtime and achievement progress close at hand. Can’t decide what to play? Let the random game picker choose something from your collection.

Hunt achievements in style.

Follow your progress from the first achievement to the perfect game. See unlock dates and rarity, check what’s still locked, and keep personal notes on individual achievements.

Discover your next favorite game.

Explore Steam’s top sellers and most wishlisted games, revisit your own wishlist, and catch up on gaming news. A little inspiration for whatever you play next.

Your Profile data, like you've never seen.

Bring your games, achievements, perfect games, and playtime together in one profile. Explore recent activity in charts, then look back at the year with Replay.

Share your favorite milestones.

Turn a rare achievement, your progress in a favorite game, or a perfect completion into a shareable card. Customize the background, choose light or dark, and pick the stats and details that tell your story.

Make it your own.

Every part of Vapor is deeply customizable. Shape the look, layout, and details throughout the app to suit the way you play. Library and game settings are just a glimpse of the options available to make Vapor your own.

And much more...

  • Pin achievements to keep your goals close.
  • Protect hidden games with Face ID or Touch ID.
  • Control achievement spoilers and locked icons.
  • Add your favorite gaming news feeds.
  • Choose your accent color and app icon.
  • Browse reviews from other Steam players.
  • View screenshots and trailers in full screen.
  • See live player counts for released games.
  • And many more features, with more added all the time.
